### Notes
- Employer ads land on the homepage, where the "I am hiring" path is. `https://energytalentsearch.com/employer-solutions/` is a closer match if you want to skip the fork.
- Meta has no "Start a Search", "See How We Hire" or "Start Your Job Search" buttons. Those phrases stay inside the creative, and the closest native button is listed above.
- Concept C is employment-related. In Ads Manager, declare the **Employment** special ad category for it, which limits age, gender and ZIP targeting. To be safe, check whether your employer-facing campaigns need it too.
